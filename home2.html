Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We have answers.

What documents do I need to rent a car?
You need a valid Indian driving license, Aadhaar/PAN card for identity proof, and a credit/debit card for payment. International customers need a passport and an IDP.
Can I extend my rental period?
Yes! Log in to your customer dashboard and submit an extension request. Subject to vehicle availability, we process extensions within a few hours.
Is fuel included in the rental price?
No, our rentals follow a full-to-full fuel policy. You receive the car with a full tank and return it with a full tank. This keeps things simple and fair.
What happens if the car breaks down?
Call our 24/7 helpline immediately. We will dispatch roadside assistance and arrange a replacement vehicle if needed, at no extra charge.
Can I drive the car outside my home state?
Yes, with prior approval. Additional interstate permits may apply and will be arranged by us. Please inform us at the time of booking.
