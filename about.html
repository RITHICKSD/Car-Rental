Our Story & Team

Driven by passion, guided by trust — the people and values behind DriveFree.

How It All Started

DriveFree was born in 2018 in the heart of Mumbai, when our founder Vikram Nair struggled to find a reliable, affordable self-drive rental service for a family road trip. Tired of opaque pricing and poor vehicle quality, he set out to build something better.

Starting with just 5 vehicles in a single garage, DriveFree has grown into a trusted fleet of 40+ premium vehicles operating across 12 cities in Maharashtra and beyond. Our secret? We treat every rental like we are handing the keys to a family member.

Today, DriveFree serves over 5,000 happy customers and is the go-to choice for weekend getaways, business trips, and everything in between.

Mission & Vision

The principles that guide every decision we make

Our Mission

To make self-drive car rentals accessible, affordable, and enjoyable for every Indian traveller — by providing a transparent, technology-powered rental experience backed by a well-maintained fleet and exceptional customer service.

Our Vision

To become India's most trusted self-drive rental brand by 2030, empowering millions of people to discover the country on their own terms — with safety, confidence, and freedom at every turn of the road.
